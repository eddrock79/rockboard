# Home Ritual artwork

- `cathedral.svg`: original cathedral, rose-window, candle and skull-mitre linework created for this home jukebox.
- `ghost-wordmark.webp`: Ghost's wordmark, downloaded from the band's official website for this personal home-use theme. Source: https://ghost-official.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/ghost-logo-updated-1.webp (https://ghost-official.com/). The wordmark belongs to its respective rights holders. This is an unofficial fan-made jukebox theme.

Assets are served locally with the jukebox. There are no third-party image requests during use.

## Landscape theatre

- `ghost-cathedral.webp`: original panoramic cathedral illustration generated for this jukebox with the built-in image generation tool. Ghost artwork and animation belong to their respective rights holders and are included for the requested private home-use fan jukebox. They are visual scenes; the existing local player remains the only music source. Only the selected video loads, and motion can be stopped. The board respects reduced-motion preferences.

### Cathedral generation prompt

Built-in image generation; the project asset is `home-site/assets/ghost-cathedral.webp`.

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original panoramic background artwork for a private Ghost-the-band themed home music jukebox, to be animated later with code. Create a spectacular dark gothic cathedral interior as a cinematic painted album-art world, widescreen 16:9. A monumental skeletal papal effigy in an elaborately embroidered black and antique-gold mitre and vestments stands in the middle-right of the composition, face like a solemn ivory skull; inspired by the theatrical Papa Emeritus universe of the band Ghost. Towering pointed arches, intricate occult rose windows, organ pipes, gilded tracery, spectral blue and teal stained glass, small crimson candles on the far left and right edges, subtle haze, tiny embers. Exquisite highly detailed gothic illustration, antique engraved textures with sumptuous dramatic volumetric lighting, beautifully dark and sinister but elegant, worthy of a dedicated Ghost fan's music room. Composition: architectural depth towards the center; skull-clergyman at roughly 65 percent across, head at 30 percent down, large but within the frame; the left third and lower quarter must remain relatively dark and uncluttered so a real album cover and readable music text can be placed there by the application. No text, no lettering, no words, no logos, no interface, no rectangles or pretend UI controls, no album sleeves, no watermarks. This is finished full-bleed illustration, not a mockup. Use rich midnight navy, desaturated petrol teal, old gold and ivory with restrained candle-red accents. Keep candles and architecture sharp; fog subtle so later animated fog layers will be visible.
